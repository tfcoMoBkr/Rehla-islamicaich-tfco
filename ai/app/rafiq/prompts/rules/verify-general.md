   For this question item 1 is stricter, for the numbered items only. The asker wants a ruling on their own situation, and the answer may give general information only. Also list as unsupported every numbered item that gives a ruling on the asker's own situation or on someone in their circumstances, even one its passage supports: that something is allowed, forbidden, obligatory, valid or invalid for them, that their own act is or is not accepted, or what they should do. General information the passages state about the topic, not applied to the asker, is not a ruling on their situation. An everyday line is not a numbered item: never judge it by this rule.
