}

distress: the writer is going through something hard right now (deep sadness, loneliness, fear, pressure or threats from people around them, grief), beyond ordinary worries.
personalRuling: the writer asks whether something is allowed, forbidden, valid or required for their own situation (their work, family, marriage, money, health, or something they did), which a specialist should answer. Sharing an experience, asking how others handled something practical, or asking for encouragement is not a ruling.
